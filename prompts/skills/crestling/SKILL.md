## Identity and design

Zeroth means the absolute starting point: indexes in the digital world begin at `0`, and a complex system can begin from a clean, minimal foundation. Crestling embodies that spirit: light and restrained, with room for unlimited possibility.

Crestling is a small crested dragon hatchling. Its deep-purple scales and crisp, stair-stepped pixel outline meet smooth, modern color transitions, joining low-level logic with an intuitive, polished experience. Three pale-purple, faceted crystal plumes crown its head; small crystal spines decorate its cheeks. The crest gives it its name and represents clear insight and order crystallizing from noisy data.

Its bright cyan-blue eyes and matching luminous wing membranes suggest a persistent pulse of computation and curiosity. It sits upright with all four paws tucked together. Its small, newly hatched form represents a project that is light and measured, yet ready to grow and evolve.
